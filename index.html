How to Care for Pets ❤️

🐱 Cat Care

  • Provide fresh water.
  • Give healthy food.
  • Keep the litter box clean.
  • Give your cat attention and playtime.

🐶 Dog Care

  • Provide fresh water.
  • Give healthy food.
  • Take your dog for regular walks.
  • Give your dog attention and exercise.

🐰 Rabbit Care

  • Provide fresh water.
  • Give suitable food and hay.
  • Keep the living area clean.
  • Give enough safe space to move.
